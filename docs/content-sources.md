## Editorial decisions

- Preserve the emergency instruction: call 999, ask for Police, then Mountain Rescue.
- Do not invent current member counts, fundraising totals or performance statistics.
- Use the general charity JustGiving page instead of presenting the old 2022 building campaign as a current appeal.
- Omit obsolete Amazon Smile donation information.
- Treat the September 2026 challenge as past; do not create a future date or open registration.
- Do not describe generated images as authentic rescue photography.
- Route real enquiries to existing team channels rather than a non-functional submission form.
